Sound familiar?

Raising a young child comes with quiet questions every day

Between camera rolls, school chat groups, and well-meaning advice, it is easy to feel overwhelmed.

“Is this normal for her age—or should I ask our doctor?”

You have hundreds of cute videos on your phone, but when your pediatrician asks when a skill started, it’s hard to piece the timeline together.

“What did he actually do at preschool today?”

Teachers care deeply, yet writing daily notes for every child takes hours away from the classroom—leaving parents with only brief updates.

“Which sensory class or speech clinic is truly right for us?”

Searching across social media and messaging ten different centers just to check open slots and pricing takes time you don’t have.

Pernahkah Anda merasakannya?

Membesarkan buah hati selalu hadir bersama pertanyaan di setiap hari

Di antara tumpukan galeri ponsel, percakapan grup sekolah, dan beragam nasihat sekitar, wajar jika orang tua kerap merasa bimbang.

“Apakah ini wajar untuk usianya—atau sebaiknya saya tanyakan ke dokter anak?”

Ada ratusan video menggemaskan di ponsel Anda, tetapi saat dokter anak menanyakan kapan suatu kemampuan baru pertama kali muncul, sulit menyusun kembali garis waktunya.

“Apa saja sebenarnya yang ia lakukan di prasekolah hari ini?”

Guru sangat menyayangi anak-anak, namun menulis laporan harian untuk setiap murid memakan banyak waktu—sehingga orang tua sering kali hanya menerima kabar singkat.

“Kelas sensorik atau klinik wicara mana yang benar-benar tepat untuk anak kami?”

Mencari di media sosial dan menghubungi puluhan tempat hanya demi mengecek jadwal yang tersedia serta biaya memerlukan waktu yang tidak sedikit.

How booking a class or consultation works in 4 simple steps

1. Browse & Pick a Slot

See real open times right in the app

Explore classes, schools, and clinics by category, read KIIDA’s site-visit notes, and choose an available session time that fits your family’s week.

2. Hold Your Spot (1-Hour Window)

Complete payment with gentle reminders

Submit your short booking form and pay within 1 hour to lock your slot (flat IDR 5,000 service fee shown upfront), with friendly reminders every 24 hours up to D-1.

3. Automatic Family Calendar Update

Synced the moment payment confirms

Once confirmed, the session appears on your KIIDA Family Calendar and the center’s remaining open slot count updates automatically.

4. Protected Partner Settlement

Peace of mind before session day

KIIDA holds your booking payment securely and transfers funds to the learning center or clinic 24 hours before your scheduled session.

About KIIDA

Built to walk beside families in every early step.

KIIDA is an early-childhood development companion created in Jakarta by PT Kiida Tumbuh Bersama—connecting parents, teachers, learning centers, and pediatric specialists around how children grow.

Our Story

Why we started KIIDA

During a child’s first five years, growth happens everywhere: at the breakfast table, on the preschool play mat, and in sensory classes. Yet for most families, those moments end up scattered across thousands of phone photos, paper folders, and separate chat threads.

At the same time, preschool teachers spend hours writing manual logs after class, and parents who want a good sensory playgroup or speech consultation have to piece together recommendations from scratch.

We founded PT Kiida Tumbuh Bersama to bring all three sides together—turning everyday activity moments into calm, structured milestone tracking aligned with CDC guidelines, and connecting families with trusted classes, clinics, and developmental goods when they need them.

The Meaning of Our Mark

Three simple shapes that represent every child’s circle of care.

Help Center & FAQ

Answers to common questions about KIIDA.

Everything you need to know about the Growth Diary, CDC milestone checklists, child privacy, and booking classes or consultations.

1. Getting Started

Who is KIIDA made for, and what ages does it cover?

KIIDA is designed for parents and guardians of young children (from 2 months through 5 years of age), early-childhood teachers, and partner learning centers or pediatric clinics.

Can I track more than one child in the same family account?

Yes. You can add multiple child profiles under a single parent account and switch between them at the top of your Growth Diary. When you upload a photo or video, KIIDA helps tag the right child profile automatically.

Can I use KIIDA even if my child’s preschool isn’t on KIIDA yet?

Absolutely. Parents can use KIIDA independently at home to upload moments, track CDC developmental milestones, ask questions in Ask KIIDA chat, and book sensory classes or clinic consultations. If your school joins later, their classroom notes can connect right into your child’s diary.

2. Milestones & How the AI Works

How does KIIDA decide when a milestone is reached?

KIIDA never marks a milestone based on a single photo. Instead, it reviews patterns across multiple uploaded photos or videos over time—and combines them with observations from your child’s teacher or doctor—before marking a milestone on your age-appropriate CDC checklist.

Is KIIDA a medical diagnosis tool?

No. KIIDA is an educational and organizational companion that helps you keep track of everyday moments and prepare a clear summary for your pediatrician. It never replaces an in-person clinical evaluation by a qualified doctor or therapist.

Does KIIDA push classes or products based on my child’s milestone checklist?

Never. One of our core product principles is that we do not make automated recommendations based on developmental assessment results. Parents browse the Discovery Platform and choose what they want for their child themselves.

3. Privacy & Child Safety

Who can see my child’s photos, videos, and Growth Diary?

Your child’s Growth Diary is private to you and the family members or educators you explicitly authorize. Teachers only see classroom uploads and session notes for their assigned students, and partner centers only see profiles of children enrolled in their sessions.

How does automatic face tagging work, and is it shared?

When you or a teacher upload an activity photo, KIIDA uses a private facial identifier strictly to tag which child is in the moment and organize their diary. It is isolated to your authorized account, never sold, and never shared with advertising networks.

How can I delete a photo or my child’s profile?

You can delete any individual post or remove an entire child profile at any time from your account settings, or by emailing privacy@kiida.app.

4. Bookings, Fees & Partner Centers

How does the 1-hour booking payment window work?

When you submit a booking form for a sensory class or clinic appointment, your slot is held while you complete payment. If payment is not completed within 1 hour, KIIDA sends a gentle reminder every 24 hours up until the day before the session (D-1).

What is the IDR 5,000 booking service fee?

Each confirmed booking on the Discovery Platform includes a flat IDR 5,000 service fee, clearly shown before checkout. It covers real-time slot reservation, Family Calendar synchronization, and payment protection.

How do learning centers and clinics receive their payouts?

Once a parent’s booking payment is confirmed, the slot is locked on the partner’s Web Dashboard and the payout is transferred automatically 24 hours before the scheduled session date.
