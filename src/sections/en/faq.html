Help Center & FAQ

Answers to common questions about KIIDA.

Everything you need to know about the Growth Diary, CDC milestone checklists, child privacy, and booking classes or consultations.

1. Getting Started

Who is KIIDA made for, and what ages does it cover?

KIIDA is designed for parents and guardians of young children (from 2 months through 5 years of age), early-childhood teachers, and partner learning centers or pediatric clinics.

Can I track more than one child in the same family account?

Yes. You can add multiple child profiles under a single parent account and switch between them at the top of your Growth Diary. When you upload a photo or video, KIIDA helps tag the right child profile automatically.

Can I use KIIDA even if my child’s preschool isn’t on KIIDA yet?

Absolutely. Parents can use KIIDA independently at home to upload moments, track CDC developmental milestones, ask questions in Ask KIIDA chat, and book sensory classes or clinic consultations. If your school joins later, their classroom notes can connect right into your child’s diary.

2. Milestones & How the AI Works

How does KIIDA decide when a milestone is reached?

KIIDA never marks a milestone based on a single photo. Instead, it reviews patterns across multiple uploaded photos or videos over time—and combines them with observations from your child’s teacher or doctor—before marking a milestone on your age-appropriate CDC checklist.

Is KIIDA a medical diagnosis tool?

No. KIIDA is an educational and organizational companion that helps you keep track of everyday moments and prepare a clear summary for your pediatrician. It never replaces an in-person clinical evaluation by a qualified doctor or therapist.

Does KIIDA push classes or products based on my child’s milestone checklist?

Never. One of our core product principles is that we do not make automated recommendations based on developmental assessment results. Parents browse the Discovery Platform and choose what they want for their child themselves.

3. Privacy & Child Safety

Who can see my child’s photos, videos, and Growth Diary?

Your child’s Growth Diary is private to you and the family members or educators you explicitly authorize. Teachers only see classroom uploads and session notes for their assigned students, and partner centers only see profiles of children enrolled in their sessions.

How does automatic face tagging work, and is it shared?

When you or a teacher upload an activity photo, KIIDA uses a private facial identifier strictly to tag which child is in the moment and organize their diary. It is isolated to your authorized account, never sold, and never shared with advertising networks.

How can I delete a photo or my child’s profile?

You can delete any individual post or remove an entire child profile at any time from your account settings, or by emailing {{site.email.privacy}}.

4. Bookings, Fees & Partner Centers

How does the 1-hour booking payment window work?

When you submit a booking form for a sensory class or clinic appointment, your slot is held while you complete payment. If payment is not completed within 1 hour, KIIDA sends a gentle reminder every 24 hours up until the day before the session (D-1).

What is the IDR 5,000 booking service fee?

Each confirmed booking on the Discovery Platform includes a flat IDR 5,000 service fee, clearly shown before checkout. It covers real-time slot reservation, Family Calendar synchronization, and payment protection.

How do learning centers and clinics receive their payouts?

Once a parent’s booking payment is confirmed, the slot is locked on the partner’s Web Dashboard and the payout is transferred automatically 24 hours before the scheduled session date.
