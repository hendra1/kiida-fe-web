Our Story

Why we started KIIDA

During a child’s first five years, growth happens everywhere: at the breakfast table, on the preschool play mat, and in sensory classes. Yet for most families, those moments end up scattered across thousands of phone photos, paper folders, and separate chat threads.

At the same time, preschool teachers spend hours writing manual logs after class, and parents who want a good sensory playgroup or speech consultation have to piece together recommendations from scratch.

We founded {{site.legalName}} to bring all three sides together—turning everyday activity moments into calm, structured milestone tracking aligned with CDC guidelines, and connecting families with trusted classes, clinics, and developmental goods when they need them.

🔒 Your child’s photos are never sold or used for ads

Every photo, video, and milestone note belongs to your family. We never sell family data or share it with advertising networks, and you can delete any photo or profile whenever you wish.

🧭 No pushy recommendations—parents choose

KIIDA never pushes classes or products based on your child’s milestone results. Your Growth Diary gives you clarity; you decide what to browse and book in the Discovery Platform.

🤝 Steady patterns + real human expertise

One photo is never enough to judge a skill. KIIDA waits for consistent evidence across multiple moments and pairs it with observations from your child’s teachers and doctors.

🩺 Support, never a medical diagnosis

KIIDA helps you organize observations aligned with CDC milestones so you can have richer, more informed conversations with your pediatrician or specialist.
