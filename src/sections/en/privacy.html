2. Information We Collect

  • Account & Contact Information: Parent, teacher, or partner name, email address, phone number, and role.
  • Child Profile Information: Child’s first name or nickname, date of birth (used to show the right age-group milestone checklist), and gender.
  • Activity Photos, Videos & Notes: Media uploaded by parents or authorized teachers, timestamps, child facial tags within your authorized circle, and observation or well-visit notes.
  • Booking & Transaction Records: Class or clinic session bookings, schedule preferences, and payment confirmation details when you book through the Discovery Platform.

3. Children’s Data & Parental Consent

KIIDA is built for adults (parents, legal guardians, authorized educators, and clinicians) caring for young children. Children do not create accounts or use KIIDA directly. A child profile may only be created with the explicit consent of the child’s parent or legal guardian, and classroom observations may only be shared with enrolled families authorized by the parent and school.

4. How Automatic Tagging & AI Milestone Analysis Work

When you or your child’s teacher upload a photo or video, KIIDA automatically records the timestamp and uses a private facial identifier solely to organize moments under the correct child profile. KIIDA’s AI engine reviews repeated activity evidence alongside teacher or doctor notes to help track progress against age-based CDC milestone checklists and answer parenting questions in Ask KIIDA chat.

No Diagnostic or Promotional Profiling: Facial tags and milestone records are strictly isolated to your authorized account. They are never used for third-party advertising and never used to push algorithmic product recommendations.

5. Who Can Access Your Information

  • Parents & Guardians: Full access to your child’s Growth Diary, uploads, and bookings.
  • Teachers: Access limited to uploading classroom activity moments and adding session observation notes for their assigned students.
  • Partner Learning Centers & Clinics: Access limited to managing their schedules and viewing confirmed bookings and shared session context for enrolled children.

6. Data Retention, Export & Instant Deletion Rights

In accordance with UU PDP No. 27/2022, you have the right to access, correct, export, withdraw consent for, or permanently delete your personal data and your child’s profile at any time. When you delete a photo, video, or child profile in the app—or request deletion via email—we permanently remove it from our active systems within 14 calendar days.
