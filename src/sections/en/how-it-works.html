How KIIDA Works

Three gentle steps from captured moment to confident next step

Designed to feel as natural as sharing a family photo—never like filling out medical paperwork.

Step 1 • Capture

Save moments from home & class

Post photos or short videos of your child playing, building, or trying something new. Your child’s teacher can share classroom moments too, with the date and child organized automatically.

Step 2 • Understand

See steady patterns, not one-off guesses

KIIDA looks for consistent patterns across multiple uploads—paired with notes from your child’s teacher or doctor—and maps them to age-based CDC developmental milestones.

Step 3 • Find Support

Explore classes, clinics & toys on your terms

Whenever you want extra support or enrichment, browse sensory classes, pediatric clinics, and tested learning toys with honest parent reviews and on-site evaluations from our team.
